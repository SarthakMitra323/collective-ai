Process

Three steps to a better answer.

01
You ask
Type any question — factual, technical, philosophical. Collective AI treats every question seriously, with no topic off-limits.
02
AI responds
Our model generates an initial answer, then searches the community knowledge base to find relevant insights from contributors who know the subject deeply.
03
The collective enriches it
Verified community contributions surface alongside the AI answer — nuances, corrections, real-world experience — making the response genuinely more useful.

What's inside

Built for depth,
not just speed.

Most AI tools optimise for fast. We optimise for accurate, nuanced, and human-verified.

Community-verified knowledge
Every insight surfaced has been contributed by a real person. Not web-scraped. Not hallucinated. Written, reviewed, and owned by someone accountable.
RAG-powered retrieval
We use Retrieval-Augmented Generation to search a vector database of community knowledge in real time, pulling the most semantically relevant context for every query.
Contribution leaderboard
The more you contribute, the smarter the AI gets for everyone. Top contributors are recognised publicly, creating genuine incentive to share knowledge.
Full conversation history
Every conversation is saved and searchable. Come back to a thread days later. Build on previous answers. Your knowledge compounds over time.
